Items

Drops, crafts, protection — and a rare charm to bind an adult as a companion.

Gaze Shard

Gaze Shard gazesnatchers:gaze_shard — base drop & craft ingredient

Dropped by Gaze Snatchers and babies. Used to craft the amulet, draught, and binding charm.

Gaze Ward Amulet

Gaze Ward Amulet gazesnatchers:gaze_ward_amulet — slows stealing while in inventory

Configurable via amuletStealReduction (default 0.55). Also required for the Binding Charm.

→

Recipe — Gaze Ward Amulet

4 × Gaze Shard + 1 × Amethyst Shard (cross shape).

crafting_shaped

Gaze Binding Charm

Gaze Binding Charm gazesnatchers:gaze_binding_charm — tame an adult Gaze Snatcher

Right-click a wild adult to bind it. The companion follows you (teleports if far), fights what hurts you and what you attack, no longer steals or blocks sleep, and ignores sunlight. Empty-hand right-click toggles sit / stand. A tamed adult does not split into babies on death.

→

Recipe — Gaze Binding Charm

6 × Gaze Shard + Gaze Ward Amulet + Echo Shard + 2 × Gold Ingot + Netherite Scrap.

crafting_shaped epic
