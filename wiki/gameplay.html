Gameplay

How do you survive a night with a Gaze Snatcher?

Typical loop

  1. You go to sleep at night.
  2. With some chance, a Gaze Snatcher appears in front of you.
  3. It stares. Look at it / break attention to avoid the steal.
  4. Too slow? An item vanishes from your inventory.
  5. Fighting a wild adult spawns 2–4 hostile babies.
  6. Looted shards craft the amulet, draught — and eventually the Binding Charm.

Taming (Gaze Binding Charm)

  1. Farm Gaze Shards, craft a Gaze Ward Amulet, gather Echo Shard, gold, and netherite scrap.
  2. Craft the Gaze Binding Charm (see Items).
  3. Right-click a wild adult with the charm — heart particles, item consumed.
  4. The companion follows you, teleports when far, and defends you in combat.
  5. Empty-hand right-click toggles sit / stand.
  6. Tamed adults no longer steal, block sleep, burn in sunlight, or split into babies.

Configuration

File created on first launch: config/gazesnatchers.json

Key Default Description
spawnChance 0.72 Chance to spawn when sleeping
ticksBeforeSteal 160 Ticks without attention before steal (~8s)
minBabiesOnSplit / maxBabiesOnSplit 2 / 4 Babies on adult death
amuletStealReduction 0.55 Slows steal if amulet is in inventory
despawnAfterSteal true Despawn after a successful steal
alwaysDropStolenFromCarrier false Carrier baby always drops the stolen item

Commands

OP / permission level 2:

Command Effect
/gazesnatchers spawn adult Spawn an adult in front of you
/gazesnatchers spawn baby Spawn a baby
/gazesnatchers trigger_sleep Simulate sleep spawn (no bed)
/gazesnatchers reload_config Reload config/gazesnatchers.json

Multiplayer

Spawn, steal, and split are fully server-side. Works on dedicated Fabric or NeoForge servers.
