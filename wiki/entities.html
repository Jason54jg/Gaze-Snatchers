Entities

Two linked creatures: the adult that watches, and the babies that attack.

Gaze Snatcher

ID: gazesnatchers:gaze_snatcher

  • Day — passive; burns in sunlight.
  • Night / sleep — chance to appear in front of a player who lies down (spawnChance, default ~72%).
  • Gaze — stands still and stares. Fail to break attention soon enough (ticksBeforeSteal, default 160 ticks ≈ 8s) and it steals an item.
  • After steal — may despawn (despawnAfterSteal).
  • On death — splits into 2–4 babies; one may inherit the stolen item.
  • Loot — 0–2 Gaze Shards when killed by a player.

Summon: /summon gazesnatchers:gaze_snatcher

Baby Gaze Snatcher

ID: gazesnatchers:baby_gaze_snatcher

  • Always hostile, faster than the adult.
  • Mostly spawn from the adult’s death split (2–4 per config).
  • Drop: 1–3 Gaze Shards.
  • The “carrier” baby may return the stolen item on death (config-dependent).
  • Quad model: large head, short body, four stubby legs, purple emissive eyes.

Summon: /summon gazesnatchers:baby_gaze_snatcher
